## Project Proposal

Prepare a presentation (~10-12 slides).

Your presentation should contain:

1. **Motivation & Problem Statement** — What problem are you solving and why does it matter? 
   Fold in why this is relevant now.

2. **Literature Survey** — Summarize 4-6 key related works. Explicitly call out:
   - What gap or limitation in existing work you're addressing
   - Why existing solutions are insufficient/challenging to apply here
   (Include a references slide with proper citations.)

3. **Novelty/Contribution** — One or two lines: is this a new method, a novel application, 
   an empirical/comparative study, or a reproduction with extensions? Be upfront about scope.

4. **Hypotheses** — State 2-3 clear, falsifiable hypotheses (not more, not vague ones).

5. **Experiments** — For each hypothesis:
   - What experiment(s) will test it
   - What environment/dataset/testbed you'll use
   - What baseline(s) you'll compare against
   - What metric(s) define success (reward, sample efficiency, regret, etc.)

6. **Resources Required** — Compute (GPU/CPU, hours), libraries/frameworks, datasets/environments, 
   and confirmation you have access to what you need.

7. **Risks & Fallback Plan** — What could go wrong (compute limits, non-convergence, time), 
   and what's your minimum viable outcome if the main hypothesis doesn't pan out?

8. **Timeline** — Week-by-week plan from proposal to final submission, with milestones.
